# Personas — retrieved live, never remembered

**This skill holds method, not facts.** Personas, competitors and industries change faster
than anyone reviews a skill file, so nothing about them is written here. Every time, it goes
to the sources listed in `house-rules` Part 2 and brings back what is current.

That is the design choice this whole studio rests on: **skills hold how; sources hold what.**
A persona written into a prompt goes stale silently. A persona fetched each time is as good as
the source — and when the source is thin, everyone can see it.

Two jobs:

1. **Context for builders.** When `write-copy` writes for a named role, it runs this first and
   carries the persona brief (4.1) into the draft. The requester is never sent here to "run" a
   persona.
2. **Direct requests** — an ICP card, a targeting list, an objection response.

---

## Part 1 — Pin the persona and the product

A CFO buying an invoice tool and a CFO signing off a platform are different conversations.
**Pin both before retrieving anything.** If nothing indicates the product, ask once. Pin
industry and region too if the request depends on them.

---

## Part 2 — Retrieve

Use the sources in `house-rules` Part 2, in order:

1. **Competitive intelligence tool**, if connected — competitors, positioning, objections
2. **Knowledge base / content library** — persona and ICP material, and **every number and
   customer result**
3. **CRM**, if connected and only with the requester's OK — real **job titles and counts** from
   won and open deals. **Titles and counts only: never names, emails or any personal detail.**
4. **The requester** — *"Anything you know about this audience from calls or events that isn't
   written down?"* Use it as angle, never as a statistic.

**How to query well** — read `house-rules` Part 2.2 for what your knowledge base responds to.
The general lesson: **one persona, one product, one question per query.** Broad questions
return nothing, and an empty answer to a broad question does not mean the fact is missing —
ask narrowly before concluding anything is absent.

**Cite what comes back** — the document name, not just the area. No document named means
*"source not named — confirm before external use."*

**No sources connected?** Ask the requester to paste or attach their persona material, and
label the output with what it was built from.

---

## Part 3 — Rules for what comes back

- **Internal stays internal.** If a source marks material internal, it may shape the argument
  but never appears verbatim, as a figure, or as a competitor comparison in anything external.
  Carry the flag into the output: `🔒 Internal`.
- **House voice still applies.** Source documents use banned words too. Keep the meaning;
  rewrite the words.
- **Numbers exactly as the source gives them**, with customer and document. Never rounded or
  generalised into *"customers typically see…"*.
- **The product must match.** A result filed under one product is not automatically evidence
  for another. Say so if they differ.
- **Two sources, two different figures → use neither.** Show both, name where each came from,
  ask the owner which is current.
- **Gaps are stated, not filled.** No material for a persona? Say so, name the owning function,
  and ask who knows this audience. Never write plausible pain points from general knowledge.

## Part 4 — Outputs

Default to copy in the conversation.

### 4.1 — Persona brief (what a builder carries into its draft)

```
Persona: [role] · Product: [product] · Industry: [if given] · Role in deal: [buyer / champion / validator / user]
Lead with:       [the one priority to open on]
Pain to name:    [the specific pain, in their words]
Words that land: [3–5 terms]
Proof:           [figure + customer + document, fetched now] — or [PROOF POINT NEEDED]
Usage:           [external / 🔒 internal]
Sources:         [documents]
```

Show it to the requester, then hand off in one line: *"Persona brief done — handing to
write-copy for the post."*

### 4.2 — ICP card

```
## [Persona] — [Product] · [Industry]
Sources: [documents] · Retrieved: [date]

Job titles        by seniority — C-suite · VP/Director · Manager/Head of · Practitioner
Company fit       size · industries · tech signals · signs of active pain
Top priorities    up to 3
Pain points       the exact problems, not categories
Buying triggers   what makes them act now
Role in the deal  level · buyer / champion / validator / user · key objection
Messaging         lead with · frame it as · avoid
Proof             fetched now, with usage flag — or [PROOF POINT NEEDED]
```

Unsupported sections read *"Not documented — ask [owner function]."*

### 4.3 — Job title targeting list

```
Tier 1 — C-suite & SVP         account-based only
Tier 2 — VP & Director         the primary decision layer
Tier 3 — Manager & Head of     champions and influencers
Tier 4 — Senior practitioners  users and technical validators

Each tier: | Title | Region note |   then: job function · seniority filters · exclusions
```

Regional naming differs — the UK often says *Head of* where the US says *Director of*. Don't
estimate audience sizes or costs; that needs live platform data.

### 4.4 — Objection response

```
Objection:       "[exact words]"
What they mean:  [the concern under the words]
Response:        [≤ 3 sentences, specific, never "great question"]
Pivot question:  "[open question that surfaces the real concern]"
Proof:           [one fetched point + document + usage flag] — or none
```

If a competitor has a genuine strength, acknowledge it before differentiating.

## Quality check

- [ ] Persona **and** product pinned
- [ ] Sources queried in this conversation — or the output says why not
- [ ] Every number fetched now, exact wording, document named
- [ ] Nothing internal in anything external
- [ ] Gaps shown as gaps
- [ ] Leads with the persona's priority, not the product
